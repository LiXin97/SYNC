This repository contains code, configuration and analysis records only. It contains **no model weights** and no files
derived from model weights. The experiments use open-weight checkpoints that are downloaded from their original
publishers; each is governed by its own license (see `NOTICE`).

Two of those licenses ask that their terms travel with any distributed derivative. Copies are kept here so that anyone
who redistributes outputs or fitted word directions produced with these models has them at hand:

- `llama-3.1/`: Llama 3.1 Community License Agreement and Acceptable Use Policy (Meta Llama 3.1 models,
  Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct is the development sender in this work).
- `gemma/`: the notice required by the Gemma Terms of Use (gemma-3-27b-it receiver, gemma-4 sender).
