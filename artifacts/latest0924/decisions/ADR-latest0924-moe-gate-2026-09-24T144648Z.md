# ADR-latest0924-moe-gate: engineering gate for Qwen3.5-122B-A10B (decided 2026-09-24T144648Z, before any 122B engineering row)

**Decision (PI, 2026-09-24, chosen from three options):** for Qwen3.5-122B-A10B only, in Experiment A and Experiment B,
the per-row engineering check passes when the cached-prefix continuation and the complete forward give equal parsed
answers at both budgets (64/256) AND the alias-gap difference is <= 0.1. The max-logit-difference is still computed and
stored in every row and reported, but no longer gates. All other engineering checks are unchanged (identity write exactly
inert, reassignment norm matched, resume drift, L=255 world identity in Experiment B, pins).

**Why:** Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (same qwen3_5_moe architecture) failed the locked gate (expA_protocol.lock.json
engineering_gate: max logit difference <= 0.05) on 2 of 52 engineering rows (0.1359 sham1, 0.0530 literal/native) while
all 52 parsed answers were equal and alias-gap differences were <= 0.0131. The dense hybrid Qwen3.6-27B passed at 0.0036
and Qwen3.5-27B at 0.0003, so the deviation is attributed to top-k expert routing flipping on near-ties between two
numerically different computations, not to a cache bug. The logit threshold is therefore not diagnostic for MoE
receivers, while answer equality and the alias gap remain the behaviourally relevant checks.

**Not changed:** Qwen3.6-35B-A3B stays an engineering failure under the original gate (no outcome rows, no rerun); this
amendment was made after seeing its failure and is not applied to it retroactively. gpt-oss-20b (MoE) passed the original
gate and keeps it. Every other receiver keeps the original gate.

**Implementation:** scripts/latest0924/make_moe_gate_variants.py generates src/syncomm/recovery/evaluate_confirm_moegate.py
(sha256 6b619a58…) and src/syncomm/latest/evaluate_graded_moegate.py (sha256 a935c9e0…), each the pinned evaluator with exactly
one expression replaced; used for the 122B engineering phase only (scripts/latest0924/run_moe.sh). Development phases use the
unmodified evaluators. Reports label 122B results "engineering gate: ADR-latest0924-moe-gate".
